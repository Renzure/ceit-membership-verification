History
The university grew out of Don Severino Agricultural College in Indang, Cavite. Republic Act No. 8468 converted it into Cavite State University in 1998. Today CvSU has its main campus in Indang and campuses around the province, including Rosario, Cavite City, Silang, Trece Martires City, and General Trias.
Vision
To be the leading university in historic Cavite, recognized around the world for strong character formation, academics, research, innovation, and service to communities.
Mission
To offer excellent, fair, and relevant education in the arts, sciences, and technology through quality teaching and responsive research and development, producing skilled and morally upright graduates ready to compete globally.
